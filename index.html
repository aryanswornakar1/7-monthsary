A letter from my heart

To My Girl,

Seven months.

It might sound like just a number, just another little milestone on the calendar… but to me, seven months means so much more. It means seven months of having you in my life, seven months of getting to know you a little more, seven months of memories, conversations, laughter, little arguments, late nights, silly moments, and countless reasons to fall for you all over again.

And somehow, even after all this time, I still look at you and think, how did I get so lucky? ❤️

You are so much more than just someone I love. You have become such a special part of my world, a part of my everyday thoughts, my happiness, my plans, and my heart. I don't love you only for the beautiful things everyone else can see. I love the little things too—the things that make you you.

The way you talk. The way you react to things. Your cute little habits. Your moods. Your laughs. Your random moments. Even the parts of yourself you sometimes don't think are worth loving.

I wish you could see yourself through my eyes just once. Maybe then you'd understand why I fell for you so deeply.

I am fully and totally obsessed with you—not in a way that can be explained by words, but in the way where somehow the smallest things about you can make my entire day better. ❤️

And honestly, I don't want these feelings to be temporary. I don't want you to be just a beautiful chapter in my life. I want to keep making memories with you. I want more months, more late-night conversations, more stupid jokes, more pictures, more little moments, more everything.

Seven months have passed, and if I had the chance to go back to the beginning and choose again…

I'd still choose you.
Every single time.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for letting me love you. Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life.

I feel incredibly blessed to have you, and no matter how many months pass, I hope you never forget this:

You are loved.
You are precious to me.
You are special to me.
And my heart still chooses you.

Happy 7 months, my love. ❤️
